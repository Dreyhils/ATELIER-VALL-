OUR MANIFESTO

Craftsmanship as an Act of Restraint

Founded in Paris in 2019, ATELIER VALLÉ was born out of resistance against the noisy, disposable nature of modern fashion.

Every garment is an architectural composition. We collaborate directly with century-old family mills in Biella, Florence, and Porto, using exclusively single-origin traceable wool, organic mulberry peace silk, and recycled cashmere.

100% GOTS organic silk and mulesing-free virgin merino wool sourced directly from ethical certified farms with complete transparency down to the flock.
Our pattern cutters utilize mathematical nested layout algorithms that reduce textile offcut waste to under 1.5%, far exceeding industry standards.
Every VALLÉ garment includes complimentary tailoring adjustments and lifetime repair services at any of our four global flagships.
